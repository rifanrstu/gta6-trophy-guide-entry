    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions — which chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order — and the roadmap structure the cited guides measured at launch: one playthrough, no difficulty setting, no online trophies, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. Sources: PowerPyx Trophy Guide & Roadmap; GTABase 100% checklist; Push Square Trophy Guide.

    Rules that protect the run

    • Manual save at every chapter start, and one before the final mission. There is no mission replay, so saves are your only way back. (PowerPyx; GTABase)
    • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends. It is the one reported missable in the whole list — see Missables.
    • Cheats off for a trophy run. Launch guides report that turning on a cheat disables trophies and saving. (GTABase; PowerPyx via GTA-Zone summary)
    • Collect as you travel. Scan vehicles, study animals, grab cards and clear WAiNK spots during the story — the 100% checklist counts all of it later.

    Missables — flagged from the official descriptions

    A trophy is flagged here only where its official description points at a one-time window, or a cited guide reports one. Everything else is labelled honestly instead of being dressed up as missable.

    Protected by a save, not truly missable

    Every story trophy unlocks automatically when its named mission or chapter is completed, and guides report story trophies cannot be missed. They are still listed in the checklist with their chapter, because with no mission replay a per-chapter manual save is cheap insurance — and the final-mission save protects the choice-shaped trophies near the end, such as Actions Speak Louder (“Choices made (for better or worse)”).

    Partner trophies — do them while both heroes are around

    Hot Together and Retail Therapy both say “with your partner” in their official descriptions. Attempt them in free roam while both protagonists are available and together, rather than assuming a post-story moment will suit them.
